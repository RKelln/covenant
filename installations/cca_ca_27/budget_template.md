### Ars Electronica Travel

Yes, Ars Electronica travel can be considered if it is framed as directly tied to Covenant's development during the grant period.

Strong framing:
- Research and development travel to study relevant installation and interface practices.
- Meetings with potential partners, presenters, or technical collaborators tied to Covenant Terminal or gallery installation development.
- Prototype demonstration, feedback gathering, or professional development that materially advances the project described in the application.

Weak framing:
- General festival attendance with no clear project-related purpose.
- Travel for a separate project not described in this application.
- Costs outside the grant period.

Recommended expense lines for Ars Electronica travel:
- Personnel Travel
	- Airfare and local transport to Austria for artist and any essential collaborator.
- Per Diem and Accomodations
	- Hotel/apartment and daily meal allowance during project-related stay.
- Insurance, transportation, freight, baggage packing and crating
	- Travel insurance, checked baggage, shipping of small prototype/demo materials if needed.
- Registration fees for professional development
	- Festival pass, accreditation, or conference registration if applicable.

Recommended revenue lines if support comes from Ars Electronica:
- Fees, guarantees, royalties, presenter contribution, distribution, exhibition rental
	- If they pay artist fee, invite fee, or travel contribution.
- Co-productions
	- If there is a formal co-production relationship.
- In-kind Support (Private and public sector)
	- If accommodation, accreditation, or technical support is provided instead of cash.
