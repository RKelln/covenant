## Draft Positioning Notes

- This budget is for creation and development, not for a fully produced exhibition budget.
- Austria travel is framed as project research, partner development, and prototype feedback tied to Covenant, not as general festival attendance.
- If presenter or foundation support is not realistic for this application, those lines can be moved into Applicant contribution or the Canada Council request can be increased.
- If you want a more conservative version, the easiest cuts are travel, equipment rental, and some external technical fees.
