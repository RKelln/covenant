## Draft Positioning Notes

- This budget is for creation and development, not for a fully produced exhibition budget.
- Austria travel is framed as project research, partner development, and prototype feedback tied to Covenant, not as general festival attendance.
- The budget assumes borrowed equipment and free testing space, so those costs are carried as in-kind support instead of cash expenses.
- Safety-related costs are held at $0 until a confirmed installation context requires signage or similar measures.
- The budget is intentionally artist-heavy so the jury can see most of the money going to writing, sound, software, and collaborator labor.
- The template has no dedicated contingency line, so the artist fee line carries a small built-in contingency reserve rather than showing a separate field.
