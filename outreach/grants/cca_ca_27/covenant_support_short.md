# Covenant

Covenant is a participatory installation that treats AI governance as a public cultural act rather than a private institutional process. Visitors encounter the work through projection, voice performance, document comparison, and an AI-mediated terminal where they can read, question, and propose amendments. The work is structured in two registers: Ritual (spoken/poetic) and Specification (normative/precise). The installation makes governance language experiential, contestable, and collectively authored.

## Installation Loop

- Visitors encounter projected Ritual text and moving image.
- At the Oath Station, they speak the text aloud and contribute recorded voice to the soundscape.
- At the Covenant Terminal, they read, question, and propose amendments to the text.
- Accepted amendments return to the projected rotation and can later be spoken by other visitors.
- The work becomes a visible public cycle: read, question, amend, speak.

## Core Components

### Primary Projection Wall

Stanza-based Ritual text and moving image form the main visual anchor of the room. The projected text is paced for reading aloud and scaled to function as both interface and performance score.

### Oath Station

A central microphone invites visitors to speak the projected text. Speech-to-text moderation and audio cleanup support participation, and the accumulated recordings gradually re-enter the gallery as part of the ambient soundscape.

### Covenant Terminal

The terminal is not a chatbot wrapper. It is a reading and contribution interface where visitors browse Covenant section by section, toggle registers, ask for context, and propose amendments through an accountable review process.

### Document Table and Material Cost Display

Printed Covenant materials are shown beside historical treaty or constitutional instruments and a display accounting for the work's physical, human, and computational cost. This grounds the installation in both historical lineage and material reality.

## Key Commitments

- Public participation is structural, not symbolic.
- AI mediation is disclosed, consent-based, and open to critique.
- Accessibility is built in through large-format text, voice interaction, keyboard-first design, and captioning/transcription support.
- Human labor, material fabrication, and computation are all made visible as part of the artwork.

## Accessibility and Consent

Each interactive station discloses how AI is used, what is recorded, how data is handled, and how participants can opt out or request deletion. The installation is designed to remain meaningful for visitors who do not record or use AI systems directly.
