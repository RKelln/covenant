# Collaborator Confirmation Letter Template

Use this template for collaborators to confirm involvement in the Canada Council application.

How to use:
- Copy this into a new file named for the collaborator (for example: `daria_confirmation.md`).
- Fill all bracketed fields.
- Collaborator can send as signed PDF, signed email, or signed letter on personal/professional letterhead.

---

[Date]

To whom it may concern,

I am writing to confirm my involvement as a collaborator on Ryan Kelln's project, Covenant, for the Canada Council for the Arts Artistic Creation application (grant period: May 1, 2026 to April 30, 2027).

## Collaborator Details

- Full name: [FULL NAME]
- Professional title(s): [TITLE(S)]
- Role in Covenant: [ROLE]
- Contact email: [EMAIL]
- Website / portfolio (optional): [URL]

## Confirmation of Involvement

I confirm that I have agreed in principle to participate in this project as [ROLE], and to contribute to the following activities:

- [ACTIVITY 1]
- [ACTIVITY 2]
- [ACTIVITY 3]

My expected contribution period is approximately [START MONTH YEAR] to [END MONTH YEAR], with flexibility based on project development needs.

## Scope Notes (Optional but Helpful)

[Add any notes about estimated hours, day rates, hourly rates, or deliverable-based fees.]

Example:
- Estimated availability: [X hours/week or X total days]
- Indicative rate: [RATE]
- Estimated deliverables: [DELIVERABLES]

## Short Bio (to be used in grant/support materials)

[Provide a 70-150 word bio written in third person. Include relevant experience, focus areas, and notable projects/publications/performances.] 

## Closing Statement

I support Covenant's development as described in this application and understand that this letter is provided to confirm my intended participation for grant assessment purposes.

Sincerely,

[SIGNATURE if applicable]

[FULL NAME]

[DATE]

---
